Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Sentinel Circle app, published by Sentinel Circle ("we"). Effective date: 2026-09-22

Summary​

Sentinel Circle runs entirely on your phone. We do not operate any server, and we never receive, store or see any of your information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The only information that leaves your phone is:

  • the alerts you set up, sent to the recipients you chose;
  • the mailbox you ask the Email sensor to check;
  • your purchase, which Google Play handles;
  • Android's own device backup, if you have it switched on.

What the app handles, and why​

Everything below is processed on your phone only. None of it is sent to us.

What leaves your phone​

  • Alerts. When a monitor raises an alert or an all-clear, Sentinel Circle sends the message text you wrote (plus the monitor and sensor name) to the recipients you configured. It uses your own phone plan (SMS and phone calls), your own mail server (email), or the WhatsApp app installed on your phone. Those services' own privacy policies apply to messages sent through them.
  • Email checking. The Email sensor connects to the mail server you entered, using the account details you gave it.
  • Purchases. Upgrading to Standard is handled by Google Play. We only learn from Google Play whether the purchase exists, so we can unlock the features. We receive no payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Android backup. If Android's automatic backup is on, Android may copy the app's settings (sensors, channels, monitors and caregiver contact details) to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. Passwords, the app-lock state and whether monitoring was running are excluded and never leave the phone. You can switch backup off in Android's settings.
  • Configuration export. If you export your configuration, the file goes where you choose to save it. Passwords are only included if you ask for them.

Special permissions​

  • Accessibility service (WhatsApp channel only). Only if you enable it, Sentinel Circle uses Android's accessibility service to press WhatsApp's Send button for an alert you configured. It acts only inside WhatsApp and only while sending that alert. It does not read, store or send any other screen content.
  • Notification access (Notification sensor only). See the table above.
  • SMS and phone calls are used only to deliver your alerts, to the numbers you entered. Sentinel Circle does not read your messages or call log.
  • Display over other apps lets an alert call start while the app is in the background.

What we share​

Nothing. We never receive your information, so we have nothing to sell or share. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Keeping and deleting your information​

All data stays on your phone until you delete it. To remove everything:

  • delete sensors, channels and monitors in the app; or
  • clear the app's storage in Android settings; or
  • uninstall the app.

Copies you made yourself (exported files, messages already sent, backups in your Google account) are removed through those services.

Security​

Passwords are kept in Android's encrypted storage. The app-lock password is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash. Connections to mail servers use encryption when your server supports it.

Children​

Sentinel Circle is a tool for caregivers and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy​

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Any change that affects what leaves your phone will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact​

Questions about this policy: Sentinel Circle — privacy@sentinelcircle.app.
